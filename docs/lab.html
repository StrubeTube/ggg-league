The rule simulator

Every slider recomputes all ten teams instantly, using the keeper selections saved on this device. Nothing here changes the real rules — it's a sandbox for arguing with Tommy. Settings save locally.

The sliders

Salary table
Flatness
Flatter → stars get cheaper to keep and trade for, late-round gems less special. Steeper → all keeper value lives in the late rounds and stars price out fast.
Team cap
How big an in-season buy can be. Lower → tighter trading, more cap jail, fire sales impossible to absorb. Higher → looser market, easier star-stacking.
Keeper budget
The redraft↔dynasty dial. Higher → more and better players kept, thinner draft, less turnover. Lower → harsh keep-or-release choices, richer draft.
Max keepers
Mostly caps how many cheap fliers a rebuilder can hoard — the budget governs real value.
Keeper slotting
Off the board → clean draft, keeping priced in dollars, tail picks consumed. Round-slot (the old way) → keeping a stud costs your actual early pick; self-balancing but brutal at the top.
Budget mode
Guaranteed-3 lets any team keep three studs at any price — stud cores never re-enter the draft, and loaded teams sit in cap jail without caring. Familiar, but superteam-friendly.
Franchise keep (budget-exempt star)
Everyone can always keep their one guy, so the headline fire-sale piece stays put — bounded to a single player so cores can't be hoarded.
Keeper salaries count against the team cap (if off, try cap ≈ $200)
Count → heavy keeper classes pay in season flexibility (less deadline ammo). Exempt → keeping is never punished and the cap polices only the draft + trade market; needs the re-attach rule (acquired players count at full salary) to stay fire-sale-proof.
