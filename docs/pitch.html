The whole rulebook

Three numbers. That’s it.

$30 → $2
Every player’s salary = his draft round. Round 1 costs $30, round 16 costs $2. No negotiating, no math homework — the board sets the price.
The cap. No trade may leave your roster above it. Stack stars if you can fit them — the arms race has a ceiling now.
The floor. No trade may leave your roster below it. Sell whoever you want — but you must get a real team back. Tanking into a JV squad is over.
The price board

Keep a guy and he climbs one round of price per year — stars get expensive, bargains stay fun. Waivers cost $0, so nobody ever gets stuck unable to field a lineup.

Game day

How a trade works now

  1. Make your deal. Players, picks, whatever — same as always.
  2. Check the band. After the swap, both rosters must sit between $ and $.
  3. Inside? It’s live. Outside? No deal. Bring real salary back and try again — that’s the whole negotiation.

Draft over the cap on purpose? Legal — welcome to cap jail: you can still play, stream waivers, and trade your way down. You just can’t trade your way deeper in.
