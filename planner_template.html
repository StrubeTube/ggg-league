Who are you keeping?

Tap a team, toggle keepers, and watch the budget and opening cap react. Keepers come off the draft board — each keep removes your latest-round pick. Costs escalate one round-step every year kept.

League overview — projected opening caps under current selections
How the math works. A player's keep cost is his 2025 draft round moved up one step on the salary table (immediate escalation); "if kept again" shows the following year's step. Round-1 players escalate past the table at +$6 per step. Only players drafted in 2025 are keeper-eligible — undrafted pickups are rental-only. Projected opening cap = keeper salaries + the salaries of the draft rounds you still hold (your latest-round picks are removed, one per keeper, and any acquired picks beyond your roster space are ignored); empty roster slots fill from waivers at $0. Your single most expensive keeper is your franchise keep — exempt from the keeper budget but fully counted against the cap. Rosters are final 2025; the planner refreshes when the 2026 league renews on Sleeper. Selections save in your browser only.
