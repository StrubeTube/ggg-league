The rule simulator

Every slider recomputes all ten teams instantly, using the keeper selections saved on this device. Nothing here changes the real rules — it's a sandbox for arguing with Tommy. Settings save locally.

The sliders

Salary table
Flatness
Flatter → more stars kept, league drifts toward dynasty. Steeper → stars price out fast, more turnover, all keeper value hides in the late rounds.
Team cap
Lower → tighter market: fire-sale hauls impossible to absorb, more cap jail, fewer blockbusters. Higher → looser market: easier star-stacking, deadline arms races return.
Team floor
Higher → fire sales die: sellers must take real salary back ("field a real team"). Lower/off → liquidations return. Too high and rebuilders can't sell at all. Waivers never trip it.
Keeper budget
The master redraft↔dynasty dial. Higher → dynasty: better players kept, thinner draft, slower turnover. Lower → redraft: harsh keep-or-release choices, richer draft night.
Max keepers
Higher → rebuilders hold more cheap lottery tickets. Lower → more late-round talent returns to the draft. The budget, not this, governs real value.
Keeper slotting
Off the board → keeping stars gets easier (costs dollars + a tail pick, draft stays clean). Round-slot → keeping stars gets brutal (costs your actual early pick; the old way).
Budget mode
Guaranteed-3 → superteams: stud cores never re-enter the draft and loaded teams shrug off cap jail. Budget-governs-all → stars circulate and every keep is a real decision.
Franchise keep (budget-exempt star)
On → star retention up, fire-sale pressure down (everyone always keeps their one guy; bounded to a single player). Off → tighter budgets, more stars back in the draft.
Keeper salaries count against the team cap
Count → good keepers cost season flexibility (heavy classes have less deadline ammo). Exempt → keeping is never punished; the cap polices only the draft + trade market — kept players re-attach at full salary when traded, so fire sales stay priced.
Apply these rules to the Home page
While on, the Home page keeper corner and cap board use these Lab settings instead of the adopted rules, with a banner so nobody mistakes the sandbox for policy. Per device.
